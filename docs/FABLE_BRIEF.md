## Characters and files

`docs/rigs/character_manifest.json` lists every exact requested filename, original canvas size, current sprite, reference sheet, aliases, body type and status. Intended directory: `game/rig_parts/<name>/`. Those directories/PNGs do not yet exist. `head_blank`, `eyes_open`, `eyes_closed`, `mouth_closed/open/smile/sad/surprised`, `torso` are common names. Human/biped sets use upper_arm/lower_arm/hand/upper_leg/lower_leg/foot with near/far suffixes. Quadrupeds and insects use numbered upper/lower/paw sections; birds use wings and leg sections. Species additions are explicit in the manifest. Do not fabricate human hands/heels for animals that lack them.

Grandpa, Jad and Sulaiman are one identity, not three rigs. Thaaloob uses fox artwork; Shahd uses bees artwork. Anthropomorphic biped animals use the human limb topology while retaining their species design. Bat, spider, octopus and wooden boat require special handling beyond the four ordinary body types; spider is grouped with insect topology for planning, not biological classification.

## Quality rules learned from Reem

Use one intact torso sprite/layer, with original view/angle fixed across all poses. Do not rotate the torso toward the camera to fake arm motion. Rotate arms and legs around real shoulder/elbow/wrist/hip/knee/ankle pivots; do not inflate or scale limb widths. Reem's existing weighted-mesh details are an approved exception to preserve, not a reason to rewrite her.

Render the neutral assembly over a temporary 50% original overlay at exactly the original registration/scale. Blank heads must have no baked eyes/mouth; every eye state shares one anchor and every mouth shares another below the nose. Remove the reference overlay before delivery. Reconstruct occluded joint surfaces rather than extending straight chopped bitmap edges.

Build contact/down/passing/up for each side, exactly mirrored across frames 1/5, 2/6, 3/7 and 4/8. Same-side arm opposes leg; both arms pass near the body. Use eased interpolation and cadence tied to actual travel. Reem's accepted reference values are +/-25-degree thighs, +/-20-degree shoulders, 40-degree passing knee, 370.5px stride travel and +/-31.35px body bob in native coordinates; adapt to each anatomy and size, never impose human gait on wings, spider legs or tentacles. Heel-to-toe contact applies to appropriate human feet; planted paws/claws stay fixed while airborne feet tilt. Lift passing feet visibly and keep knees bending forward.

One continuous silhouette outline: no parallel torso/limb borders, black dashes, white shoulder wedges, collar remnants, jagged pixels, alpha seams, tunic-hem gaps or detached hands/feet. Maintain generous hidden overlap, torso in front of trouser tops, scarf over shoulder caps, and appropriate far/near limb order. Use smooth linear filtering and antialiased edges. Breathing/blinks/speech/expressions should preserve stable face anchors. Expressions follow existing line tone; mouth closes at voice end.

Check all eight poses at actual game size and 400% before committing. Reem evidence uses actual scale .362 and a separate render at 1.448; do not confuse nearest-neighbour source-pixel enlargement with filtered game output. **Reem's walk is final: no further changes unless a defect is visible at game size.**
